## 2026-11-10: the auditor line, corrected for Anthropic

**What the essay said.** "None of those promises comes with an independent auditor, or a penalty if your bill goes up anyway."

**What was found.** Anthropic's data center policy page says that after a site is up and running, Anthropic will publish key metrics on its impacts each year, and that those metrics will be verified by an independent third party. The page carries no date, so it is not known whether it existed when the essay was written. The February 11, 2026 announcement the research relied on mentions no outside verification. Neither page describes a penalty if a bill rises anyway.

**What changed.** A dated correction now sits under that paragraph in the essay. The original sentence was left in place so the change can be seen. The process record still shows the line as it was drafted, because the record is anchored and does not change.

**Whose miss it was.** The machine's research opened the February announcement and not the policy page. The gap was caught on November 10, while checking a pitch against Anthropic's own pages.

# Confidently Wrong About a Toilet: Process Record

The conversation behind the essay, kept as it happened, with private details taken out.

Recorded October 8, 2026. Gage Woodle and Claude (Anthropic), in one long chat, much of it in voice mode. Claude goes by Lumen in Gage's work.

## What this is

This is the receipt for the essay "Confidently Wrong About a Toilet." The essay says: "Here's mine, all three of them, with the receipts." The shield already had its own record. This file is the other two, and the drafting of the whole piece.

## What was removed, and why

This record is different from the one for The Scarlet Letter. That one is the full conversation with one detail changed. This one is not complete, on purpose.

The chat that produced this essay was mostly not about the essay. It was a working session about a real problem at my mother's house, and it holds her address, her health, her landlord, the companies involved, money, my family's private texts, and a lot of unrelated work. None of that is mine to publish, and none of it is needed to check the essay.

So here is the rule I used:

- **Kept:** every exchange that the essay is built from, or that shows where a line in the essay came from, verbatim.
- **Removed entirely:** turns about other work (an event I was producing, a video backdrop, job research, a test I am running on another company's AI). They have nothing to do with the essay.
- **Removed inside a kept turn:** private details. Each removal is marked in square brackets saying what kind of thing was taken out, like [landlord's name] or [plumbing company]. Where a stretch of a turn was cut, it shows as [...].
- **Not published at all:** the phone and Zoom recordings the essay quotes. Other people are on them who did not agree to be published. Where the essay quotes those calls, the quote is shown as Claude read it back from the transcript in this chat.
- **Names:** my mother, my sister and the landlord are not named. Jen is named because she is named in the essay. Other people are not.

This proves less than a full record. You are trusting my judgment about what was relevant. I would rather say that plainly than publish my mother's life to make the receipt look bigger.

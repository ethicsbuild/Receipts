## What the agreement says (as I read it; I'm not a lawyer)

- **Section 16.4 (Publicity):** neither party may use the other's name, brand or logo, or publicly announce that the parties have entered into the agreement, without the other's prior consent. TypeSafe may list a customer as a customer without asking.
- **No clause directly addresses** benchmarking, evaluating or publishing performance results.
- **Section 14.1** counts "non-public information with respect to the Services" as TypeSafe's confidential information. Whether a customer's own measurements of the model fall under that is unclear to me.
- **Section 4.2** assigns Output to the customer.

## Where it stands

Consent was requested three times and not given. The company declined to review. The protocol will be run once, privately, with the results sealed. If TypeSafe consents, or I get clear legal footing, I'll publish the result either way, with this record attached. If not, nothing is published.

# The Janky Cart: Process Record

The conversation behind the essay, kept as it happened, with names and private details taken out.

Recorded November 1 to 4, 2026. Gage Woodle and Claude (Anthropic), in one chat on the Claude mobile app, most of it spoken aloud on November 1. Claude goes by Lumen in Gage's work.

## What this is

This is the receipt for the essay "The Janky Cart," about a hand-built Minecraft golf cart, the perfectionist Gage used to be, a child's story about zip ties, and what handmade things are worth once machines can make anything perfect. This record shows where each part of the essay came from, the seed notes it grew from, every source opened, the draft as first delivered, and the places where the machine got something wrong.

## What was removed, and why

One chat fed this essay. The first day was Gage talking through a weekend out loud, the day after Halloween. Much of that day was personal, and much of it is not in the essay.

The rule:

- **Kept:** every exchange the essay is built from, verbatim.
- **Replaced with a role in brackets:** every name except Gage's. His friend who asked the question, the friend whose daughter told the zip-tie story, the daughter, Gage's wife, children and mother, and two other friends.
- **Removed inside a kept turn:** family details, details of the friend's last years beyond what the essay says, a detail about the daughter's family, a description of her father beyond "gruff," a business matter, the name of a school in Gage's past, and one reference to work of Gage's that is not published under his name. Each is marked in square brackets.
- **Kept at Gage's direction:** the exchange about his agreement with his wife and his mother to limit these conversations (turns 42 to 46), and the stretch on faith, where technology is headed, and a check-in about his rest and state of mind (turns 51 to 62). Roles stand in for names. He wants readers to see this part of the journey.
- **Summarized:** the turns on November 4 about formatting the essay for Substack.
- **Left out:** the machine's tool calls (searches of Gage's earlier chats, memory reads, web searches, document edits), except where a document it delivered is the thing this essay grew from. Those are reproduced whole.

This proves less than a full record. You are trusting my judgment about what was relevant.
